How to Play

Use your hand in front of the camera to draw smooth strokes in the air.

  • ☝️ Drawing: raise only index finger.
  • 🤏 Grab: pinch thumb + index to move strokes.
  • ✋ Erase: open palm to erase nearby strokes.
  • ✊ Idle: close fist to pause interaction.
